## What is CloudZero?

CloudZero is a cloud cost intelligence platform that helps engineering connect
technical decisions to business results:

- [Cost Allocation And Tagging](https://www.cloudzero.com/tour/allocation)
  Organize and allocate cloud spend, increase tagging coverage, or work on
  showback.
- [Kubernetes Cost Visibility](https://www.cloudzero.com/tour/kubernetes)
  Understand Kubernetes spend alongside total spend across containerized and
  non-containerized environments.
- [FinOps And Financial Reporting](https://www.cloudzero.com/tour/finops)
  Operationalize reporting on metrics such as cost per customer, COGS, and
  gross margin. Forecast spend, reconcile invoices, and investigate variance.
- [Engineering Accountability](https://www.cloudzero.com/tour/engineering)
  Foster a cost-conscious culture where engineers understand spend and get
  faster feedback.
- [Optimization And Reducing Waste](https://www.cloudzero.com/tour/optimization)
  Focus on reducing spend by finding waste, inefficiencies, and discounting
  opportunities.

Learn more at [www.cloudzero.com](https://www.cloudzero.com/).
